Call for papers

Bring evidence. Take a seat on stage.

We invite short papers of up to four pages providing evidence for or against either motion. Position papers, negative results and failure-case analyses are explicitly welcome. Submissions are non-archival and reviewed double-blind by at least two reviewers. Authors may indicate which motion their work supports.

No paper? Students and early-career researchers can also submit a short position statement as a junior voice.

Topics include, but are not limited to

  • Integrating foundation-model outputs (poses, depth, correspondences) into SLAM
  • Characterizing uncertainty, calibration and error correlation of learned perception for state estimation
  • Semantic and language-embedded maps for robot perception
  • Implicit, end-to-end learned state versus explicit state estimation for navigation and control
  • Introspection and out-of-distribution failure detection
  • Consistency, observability and safety guarantees of estimators with learned inputs
  • Benchmarks and datasets comparing learned and probabilistic approaches to SLAM
