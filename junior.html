Junior voices

Take a side. Make your case.

We invite junior and early-career researchers, from Master's students to postdocs, to submit a short position statement on one or both motions. No paper needed: tell us which side you are on and why. Authors of strong statements are invited to join the debates if they wish, and with the author's consent, selected statements are presented on this website and at the workshop.

Address one motion or both, each with its own side and statement.
